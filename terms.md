Last updated: 16 November 2026

Your documents are yours. FileAstra processes the files you choose on your iPhone or iPad, without uploading them to a FileAstra server. You do not need a FileAstra account, and the app does not contain advertising, third-party analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking.

This policy explains what the app handles locally, the limited circumstances in which information can leave your device, and the choices you have.

2. FILES AND INFORMATION PROCESSED ON YOUR DEVICE

FileAstra accesses the content you choose to scan, import, create, or edit so it can carry out the tools you use. This may include:

- Photos, screenshots, camera scans, PDFs, and other supported files.
- Text recognized from images or documents, PDF form entries, handwritten signatures, annotations, and edits.
- File information such as names, sizes, formats, page counts, and timestamps.
- Metadata already contained in selected files, such as camera details, capture dates, and embedded location information.
- PDF passwords you enter for a requested operation.

Scanning, text recognition (OCR), image conversion, compression, PDF editing, signing, redaction, and metadata processing run on your device. We do not receive these files or their contents through normal use of these tools. We do not upload them to an AI service or use them to train AI models.

Files can contain sensitive information, including identity documents, financial details, health information, or signatures. That information is processed as part of the content you choose; we do not maintain a central repository of it.

Embedded location information is read from selected files when relevant to the tool. FileAstra does not request access to your device's live location.

PDF passwords are used locally. FileAstra does not send them to us or store them in saved task definitions or app preferences. Unlock PDF clears its password field after a successful operation or a document change. An unlocked output is a separate copy without the original password protection.

3. LOCAL SETTINGS, FILES, AND TASKS

The app stores information locally to make its features work. This includes appearance and language preferences, cleanup and notification settings, task configurations, interrupted-task status, and the remaining balance of free task credits.

The Files tab uses local file records, including a file identifier, location, producing tool, and creation time, along with file details needed for display. These records help you find, preview, export, and delete generated files. They are not uploaded to a FileAstra account or analytics service.

Files passed through the share extension are temporarily copied into storage shared by FileAstra and its own extension. This allows the main app to receive your selected files. Shortcuts actions process the input you supply and return their result to the shortcut. Other actions you add to that shortcut may handle the result under their own rules.

4. PERMISSIONS AND YOUR CHOICES

Camera. Used when you choose to scan documents or capture images, including ID cards. Document Scanner uses the camera; it does not upload a camera feed to us.

Photos. Used to import images you select and, with permission, save finished images to your photo library. Depending on the tool and permission available, the app may read associated details such as the original filename and capture date. It does not upload or build a remote index of your photo library.

Files. Apple's document picker lets you choose files to open and locations for exported copies. A file provider may download a selected file from iCloud Drive or another service to make it available on your device.

Notifications. Optional task-completion notifications are scheduled locally. They do not require a FileAstra push-notification server.

Clipboard. When you choose a Copy action, the selected text or information is placed on the system clipboard. Other apps you paste into, and Apple continuity features where enabled, may then receive it.

You can decline optional permissions and change camera, photo, and notification permissions in your device's Settings. Features that need a denied permission will be unavailable until it is allowed. Revoking permission does not automatically delete copies already imported or exported.

For example, iCloud Photos, iCloud Drive, or another file provider may store an exported copy in the cloud according to your settings. FileAstra does not control those copies after export.

Support. If you email us, we receive your email address, any name you provide, your message, and any attachments or diagnostic details you choose to send. We use this information to answer you, investigate the reported issue, handle privacy requests, and maintain necessary records of those communications. Please avoid sending passwords, identity documents, or unredacted sensitive files unless they are needed for your request. A description or a redacted example is usually sufficient.

Support email is handled through Gmail. Google processes email under its applicable terms and privacy practices:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

Legal requirements. We may disclose information we actually hold, such as support correspondence, where required by law or necessary to establish or defend legal claims. We cannot disclose files that remain only on your device and have not been sent to us.

External services. Apple and the apps, file providers, and recipients you choose have their own privacy policies. Opening an external link also exposes the usual connection information, such as an IP address, to the destination website.

8. STORAGE, RETENTION, AND DELETION

Working files. FileAstra creates temporary inputs, previews, and outputs while processing. Cleanup can remove these files when they are no longer needed. The app also checks for older temporary files and abandoned share-extension imports using a 24-hour age threshold at relevant launch or access points. This is not a promise that every file is deleted exactly 24 hours after creation.

Active use. Cleanup may wait while files are being processed, edited, previewed, or shared. Device suspension, interrupted operations, or deletion errors can delay cleanup.

Files tab and retained copies. Some generated files remain in local app storage for later access. Files in temporary storage can disappear during cleanup or when iOS reclaims temporary space. Save important results to a destination you control. The Files tab is not a backup service.

Your deletion controls:

- Delete individual files from the Files tab, or use its delete control and confirm Purge All to remove files from the app's local output storage and working cache.
- Use the cache-clearing control in Settings to remove working files. This is separate from clearing retained copies in the Files tab.
- Enable Auto-Clean on Exit if you want working-cache cleanup when FileAstra moves into the background. This option is off by default. It does not clear every category of stored information and may wait for active work to finish.
- Delete custom Tasks you no longer need. Purging files does not reset app preferences, task definitions, or purchase entitlements.

Pending share-extension imports have their own cleanup process. Clearing the Files tab does not guarantee immediate deletion of every pending import.

Deleting the app, rather than offloading it, removes its associated local app data as managed by iOS. This does not delete originals, exported copies in Photos or Files, recipients' copies, Apple purchase records, or existing backups. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

FileAstra marks its managed temporary, retained-output, and share-import directories as excluded from device backup. Other data, such as preferences, may still be included by the operating system. Exported copies and earlier backups follow the settings and retention rules of their respective services.

We retain support correspondence only for as long as needed to handle the request, related follow-up, and any applicable legal or dispute-resolution obligations. The relevant criteria are whether the matter remains open, whether the correspondence is still needed to resolve it, and whether a legal retention requirement applies. Diagnostic reports we obtain are kept only as needed to investigate and verify fixes for the relevant issues, subject to applicable legal obligations. Apple controls retention within its own services. You can ask us to delete information you sent us. Any required retention will be explained when applicable.

Local deletion uses the operating system's file-management mechanisms. It is not a guarantee of forensic erasure from every device, backup, or external service.

9. SECURITY AND PRIVACY TOOLS

FileAstra uses iOS app sandboxing and platform storage protections to limit access to its local files. We do not operate remote document storage or a remote document-recovery service. Device security also depends on factors such as your passcode, operating-system updates, backups, and the destinations you choose for exported files.

Wipe Metadata and redaction work on generated copies. They do not erase information from your original files, prior exports, backups, or copies already shared. Removing metadata does not remove information visibly printed in an image. Review the finished copy before sharing sensitive material.

No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. We do not describe the app's local storage as an independently encrypted vault or promise that closing the app immediately destroys every copy.
